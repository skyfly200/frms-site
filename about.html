About FRMS

Discover our story, mission, and the passionate community behind our society.

Our Story

Founded in 1995 by a group of passionate mycologists and nature enthusiasts, the Front Range Mycological Society has grown from a small gathering of mushroom lovers to a thriving community of over 500 members.

What began as informal forays in the Colorado wilderness has evolved into a comprehensive organization offering educational programs, scientific research support, and community events that celebrate the fungal kingdom.

500+ Members Strong

Our Mission & Values

Education

We believe in sharing knowledge about fungi through lectures, workshops, and hands-on experiences to foster appreciation and understanding.

Conservation

We promote sustainable practices and advocate for the protection of fungal habitats and biodiversity in our local ecosystems.

Community

We build connections among people who share a passion for mycology, creating a welcoming space for all levels of interest and expertise.

Our Journey

Society Founded

1995

A small group of mushroom enthusiasts establishes FRMS with monthly meetings in Boulder.

First Annual Foray

1998

Our first organized mushroom hunt attracts 35 participants to the Roosevelt National Forest.

Educational Programs Begin

2005

We launch our first lecture series and identification workshops for the public.

Research Partnership

2012

Collaboration begins with University of Colorado mycologists on local fungal biodiversity studies.

Today

Present

A thriving community with diverse programs, research initiatives, and over 500 members.
