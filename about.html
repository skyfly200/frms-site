About FRMS

Discover our story, mission, and the passionate community behind our society.

Our Story

Founded in September 2025 by a group of passionate mycologists and nature enthusiasts, the Front Range Mycological Society brings together fungi lovers across the Colorado Front Range.

What began as a small gathering of mushroom enthusiasts has quickly grown into an active community offering forays, educational events, and a welcoming space for all levels of experience.

Based in Colorado's Front Range

Our Mission & Values

Education

We believe in sharing knowledge about fungi through lectures, workshops, and hands-on experiences to foster appreciation and understanding.

Conservation

We promote sustainable practices and advocate for the protection of fungal habitats and biodiversity in our local ecosystems.

Community

We build connections among people who share a passion for mycology, creating a welcoming space for all levels of interest and expertise.

Our Journey

Society Founded

September 2025

A group of mushroom enthusiasts comes together to establish FRMS and build a mycological community on Colorado's Front Range.

First Foray

November 2025

FRMS hosts its first organized mushroom foray, bringing people together to explore Colorado's fungi in the field.
